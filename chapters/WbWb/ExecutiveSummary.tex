\chapter*{Executive Summary}%
\label{ch:summary}

A first measurement of differential production cross sections of the process \(pp \to WWbb\) is presented in the \emph{\( \ell \)+jets} decay channel using data collected with the ATLAS detector during Run-II of the LHC at a center-of-mass energy of \(\sqrt{s} = \qty{13}{\TeV}\). 
The decay channel refers to the decay of the two \Wboson bosons, where one \Wboson boson decays leptonically into a charged lepton (muon or electron, \( \mu^\pm \) or \( e^\pm \)) and a neutrino \( \nu \), whereas the other one decays hadronically, and can be reconstructed using jets.

The process \(pp \to WWbb\) is closely related top-quark pair production, which mainly decays to \(WWbb\) in an intermediate state (a much smaller fraction decays through \(WWbs\)). 
However, also further channels contribute to the \(WWbb\) process, and include singly-resonant top-quark diagrams, \(Wtb\), diagrams without intermediate top-quarks, as well as higher-order diagrams with off-shell top-quarks (finite-width effect). These contributions are considered as background processes in top-quark pair production analyses, but at higher orders in perturbative QCD they interfere with other processes, like single-top production, and therefore cannot be unambiguously be treated theoretically, which therefore reduces the achievable precision for top-quark phenomenology using top-quark pair cross sections. In contrast, the direct measurement of \(WWbb\) production cross sections provides an alternative access to top-quark phenomenology, where those aspects are treated consistently in theory, albeit at the cost of considerably more complicated calculations.
The interference between top-quark pair production and single-top production at higher-order perturbative QCD also introduces a considerable uncertainty in searches for new physics phenomena at large masses, and the present measurement aims to reduce this uncertainty for future searches.

The analysis therefore focusses on two kinematic regions: a large phase space to constrain the interference effects and improve the modelling over a large kinematic region, and a dedicated measurement of the \(WWbb\) production cross section with a focus on the \Wboson boson and \bjet kinematic observables. As an addition, some observables related to top-quark pair physics are reported as well.

The hadronically decaying \Wboson boson is reconstructed using a jet-based algorithm, and considers a recombination of one jet, two jet and three jet topologies, and achieves an \Wboson boson reconstruction efficiency of greater than \qty{90}{\%} for transverse momenta greater than \qty{140}{\GeV}.
